Personal backup utility

Backups for the account owner’s Google Drive.

Jarvis Backup Drive 2 runs on the account owner’s computers and saves selected control-panel source files, instructions and sanitized operating records to the owner’s Google Drive accounts.

How the application works

A scheduled job creates a backup archive, uploads the same archive to designated backup folders, and verifies its size and checksum. The account owner can download an archive for recovery. Older archives are moved to Google Drive Trash according to the configured retention policy.

Google Drive access

The owner authorizes permission to view, create, edit, rename and delete Google Drive files. This permission covers the authorized Drive account. The scheduled backup job operates on the designated openclaw-backups folder.

Authorization takes place on Google’s OAuth consent page. The resulting credentials are stored privately on the owner’s computers.

Operator and contact
